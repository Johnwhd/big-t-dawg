The main risk is a confident answer that is not in the documents. Check the work every time, before it goes anywhere.

Every claim needs the page number it came from. If it cannot give the page number, leave that claim out.

  • An RFI going out. One question, the drawing, the spec section, and the answer that would unblock the work.
  • A thread coming back. What is still open, what was already answered, and which sheet it points at.
  • A submittal. What the spec requires, what they sent, and where the two conflict.
  • An addendum. What changed from the set already priced, sheet by sheet, before anyone reprices from memory.
  • Two sub numbers. What is in, what is out, the allowances, and anything left unpriced. A similar total is not a similar scope.
  • A change that has to get paid. What sits outside the contract, the field proof, the cost basis, and the days.
  • A contract against the drawings. Pay, notice, time, exclusions, and where the exhibit and the sheets disagree.
  • The note before a meeting. Cost, schedule, the risks that can still move, and the decisions needed. Tie each line to a record, then edit it before it goes up.

Keep one job in the chat, and attach the files that would change the answer.

Read every sentence you will send against the page it cites.

A quantity, a pay-app figure, or a bid number comes from the sheet, or from the person who measured it.
